Lexicographical data

Wikidata lexemes across Indic languages — counts are not coverage

The big number is hollow: 83% of the Malayalam lexemes carry no senses, and 80% are nouns — a bulk word-list import, not lexicography. And Malayalam is morphologically productive: a materialized word list is a debated model for it in the first place. Hindi and Urdu lexemes live under "Hindustani".
